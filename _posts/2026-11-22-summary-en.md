### [Lean Community Reports AI-Assisted Formal Verification of the Riemann Hypothesis](https://leanprover-community.github.io/blog/posts/riemann-hypothesis/) ⭐️ 9.0/10

A post on the Lean community website reports the formal verification of the Riemann Hypothesis using the Lean proof assistant through a collaboration between human mathematicians and AI systems. The effort reportedly relied on AI models, specifically Claude, to assist in formalizing extensive foundational components of modern mathematics required for the proof. While the announcement presents the result as machine-checked, the findings await broader independent verification across the wider mathematical and formal proof communities.

hackernews · g-b-r · Nov 21, 14:36 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50589618)

**「Context」** The Riemann Hypothesis is a central unsolved problem in number theory, with formal statements of the conjecture maintained within Lean&\#x27;s Mathlib library. On November 20, 2026, mathematician Levent Alpöge released a claimed proof spanning 17 million lines of Lean code developed with AI assistance, published as a software archive without an accompanying traditional paper.

**「Community Discussion」** Commenters debated the reliability of automated proof kernels, with some arguing that multi-kernel consistency checks make an exploitable bug exceedingly unlikely, while others cautioned that flawed proofs have historically bypassed independent verification tools. Participants also highlighted the collaborative dynamic, praising researcher guidance in successfully leveraging Claude to formalize foundational mathematical theory.
