### [FCC Grants SpaceX Conditional Approval for 1 Million Orbital Data Center Satellites](https://arstechnica.com/space/2026/11/the-fcc-has-authorized-spacex-to-deploy-1-million-satellites/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

The Federal Communications Commission granted SpaceX conditional authorization to deploy a constellation of up to 1 million Starmind orbital data center satellites. Under the order, SpaceX has a 14-year regulatory runway, requiring a single operational satellite within seven years, 100,000 operational satellites by November 2035, and the full constellation by November 2040. The conditional approval also permits SpaceX to seek waivers to dispose of retired satellites operating above 600 km into Earth disposal orbits or heliocentric orbits rather than burning them up in the atmosphere.

rss · Ars Technica · Nov 25, 18:22

**「Background」** Satellite constellations typically require multi-stage regulatory approvals from the FCC regarding spectrum allocation, orbital altitudes, and debris mitigation plans. SpaceX previously built out large-scale low Earth orbit constellations for internet communication, establishing a precedent for launching massive volumes of spacecraft.

**「Impact」** The decision provides SpaceX with long-term regulatory clearance to test the technical and economic viability of operating space-based data centers across altitudes ranging from 550 km to about 1,000 km over the next decade and a half.

### [IBM Research Details Open-Source CUGA Agent Harness for Indic Voice Applications](https://huggingface.co/blog/ibm-research/an-indic-voice-agent-with-cuga) ⭐️ 7.0/10

IBM Research detailed the development of an open-source Indic voice agent using its Configurable Generalist Agent \(CUGA\) harness to help regional language speakers access localized knowledge. The architecture treats CUGA as a text-in, text-out core wrapped in speech-to-text and text-to-speech layers, supporting cloud APIs from Sarvam AI or self-hosted models like SraVaani and Svara. To solve cross-lingual retrieval failures where English-only embedding models rendered queries in languages like Hindi meaningless, the team integrated the multilingual intfloat/multilingual-e5-large embedding model. The open-source agent and a step-by-step tutorial are available on Hugging Face.

rss · Hugging Face Blog · Nov 25, 10:22

**「Background」** Most digitized reference material is published in English, creating a barrier for regional language speakers who prefer voice interaction over text. Agent harnesses like CUGA automate model wiring, tool management, and guardrails so developers can focus on defining tools and prompts rather than building the agent loop.

**「Impact」** Developers building multilingual voice applications can use CUGA and multilingual embedding models to query English-language document stores using regional queries without needing fully translated document copies.

**Tags**: `#artificial intelligence`, `#voice agents`, `#open source`, `#machine learning`, `#natural language processing`
